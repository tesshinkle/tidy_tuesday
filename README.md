# Tidy Tuesday Challenges
Welcome to my Tidy Tuesday Challenge repository!\
In the repository you will be able to find the raw R code (few comments) that I use to go through each challenge and 
the Quarto Markdown Report I create to summarize what I created and what I took away from what I did.\

Each new challenge will have the Tuesday it is linked to in the R script title, whether it was created that week or not.\

# Basotho Wool Challenge (8-4-26)
The basotho wool challenge looks into Lesotho's wool trade from 2010 to 2024. 
It also looks at the impact of the weather months on the trade industry as well as which countries are the biggest importers at different times.
The report of the wool challenge is in the Wool Trade Report Quarto Markdown file.\
The following questions were answered for the challenge:
* Does Winter (being in the Southern hemisphere, these are the months June, July and August) impact Basotho wool quantity/volume exported and revenue?
* Which country imports the most Basotho wool at a time on average?

# Ecotourism Challenge (7-28-26)
The ecotourism challenge looked at the weather, number of tourists, and observations of Australian aninmals interact together.\
The following are the three questions answered for the challenge:
* How do observations of the different animals relate to numbers of tourists?
* How does weather affect tourism numbers in each region?
* Under which weather conditions are you most likely to observe a Gouldian finch?
